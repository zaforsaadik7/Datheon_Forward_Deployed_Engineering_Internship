# Executive AI Strategy & FY2026 Capital Roadmap

## 1. Capital Allocation & Infrastructure Budget
The Board of Directors has approved a total FY2026 AI capital expenditure (CapEx) budget of $14.5M ($14,500,000) dedicated to sovereign LLM inference clusters, multi-tenant vector infrastructure, and autonomous field engineering deployment. Phase 1 GPU cluster reservation accounts for $8.1M.

## 2. Projected Cost Savings & Margin Expansion
Full deployment of the Enterprise Multi-Tenant RAG platform and Autonomous Field Agent across Tier-2 customer support and legacy migration workflows is projected to deliver $7.2M ($7,200,000) in annualized operating expense reductions and improve gross EBITDA margin by 340 basis points by Q4 FY2026.
